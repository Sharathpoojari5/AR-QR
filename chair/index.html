Chair not appearing? Floor tips
  • Stand 1 to 2 metres back and point the camera at the floor, not the wall.
  • Move the phone slowly side to side, like painting the floor, until the chair appears.
  • Plain or shiny tiles are hard for phones. Put a mat, newspaper or a few sheets of paper on the floor, or aim where the floor meets a skirting board or furniture.
  • Use good, even light. Avoid very dim rooms and mirror-like floors.
  • Still stuck? Tap Try Google AR viewer or Quick view.

Place it in 3 steps

  1. Point the camera at the floor where it should go, from 1 to 2 m away.
  2. Move the phone slowly side to side until the chair appears.
  3. Drag it with one finger. Twist two fingers to turn it. Its size is locked to real size.

Didn't see the chair?

The phone could not find the floor. Move slowly, add light, and aim at a textured floor or the floor edge.
